# Landing Page Interview

Follow `../../copy-foundations/references/interview-protocol.md`. Ask only what is not already known.

## Round 1 (always)

Post these together, numbered, and invite shorthand replies.

1. **What type of page is it?**
   a) Opt-in (lead magnet)  b) Webinar registration  c) Book-a-call  d) Sales page  e) Thank-you page
2. **Where is the traffic coming from, and how warm is it?**
   a) Facebook cold  b) Facebook retargeting  c) Email  d) Organic
3. **What is the offer, the price (if any), and the single conversion action?** (For example: "free strategy call, no price, book on the calendar.")
4. **What platform will it be built on?**
   a) GoHighLevel  b) Other (say which)

## Round 2 (only what the answers trigger)

| Trigger | Ask |
|---|---|
| Always | **Proof:** what testimonials, results, logos or case studies are available? (Real only.) |
| Always | **Objections:** what are the top three reasons someone would not convert? |
| Always | **Structure:** any sections that must be included, or avoided? |
| Opt-in, registration, booking | **Form:** a) Short (email only or name and email)  b) Standard (name, email, phone)  c) Qualifying (adds 1 or 2 questions) |
| Always | **Extras:** any disclaimers, privacy or terms links, or legal lines that must appear? |
| Thank-you page | What should they do next, and is there a video, download or calendar to show? |
| Webinar registration | Date, time, time zone, host, and what attendees will learn. |
| Sales page | Guarantee, bonuses, payment plan, and what is included. |

## Required fields (must be answered or assumed)

Page type, traffic source and temperature, offer, the single conversion action, platform.

Nice to have: proof (default: placeholders, none invented), objections (default: derive the standard four and mark them as assumed), form length (default from the friction ladder), legal extras (default: none added, flagged as a reminder).

## Confirmation summary template

```
Here is what I will write:
- Deliverable: <page type> landing page copy
- Traffic: <source and temperature>
- Offer and price: <offer, price or none>
- One conversion action: <action>
- Platform: <platform>
- Structure: <blueprint name and length>
- Proof and objections: <what will be used>
- Form: <fields>
- Assumptions: <list, or none>

Reply yes to write it, or tell me what to change.
```
