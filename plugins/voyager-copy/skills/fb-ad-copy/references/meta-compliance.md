# Meta Ads Policy Compliance

Run the finished package (primary text, all 5 headlines, description) through this file before delivery. It is a separate pass from the headline checklist and the human-voice lint. It checks rejection risk, not copy quality. A rejected ad costs a 24 to 72 hour review cycle and repeated violations can restrict an ad account.

**Core principle:** write about the offer and the outcome, never about the viewer's personal or protected attributes, even if the targeting was built around them.

## 1. Personal attributes (the most common rejection)

Do not assert or imply anything about the viewer's race, ethnicity, religion, age, sexual orientation, disability, health condition (physical or mental), financial status, criminal record, name, or membership in a protected group. This applies even if it is true and even if the audience was selected for it.

| Bad | Good |
|---|---|
| "Are you struggling with debt?" | "Struggling businesses can turn cash flow around in 90 days." |
| "Because you're over 50, retirement planning matters more than ever." | "Retirement planning gets more urgent every year you wait." |
| "Diabetic? This supplement can help." | "Managing blood sugar doesn't have to mean giving up your favorite foods." |

Professional and business identity ("Business Owner", "Agency Owner") is allowed. Job title and business stage are not personal attributes. The line is crossed when copy reads like a diagnosis of the person's body, finances, beliefs or private life. Reword around the situation or the outcome.

## 2. Prohibited content

- Guaranteed or unrealistic results ("Guaranteed to double your revenue", "Lose 20 lbs in a week")
- Unsubstantiated claims ("#1 rated", "proven by science") the account cannot back up
- Sensationalized claims that imply one person's exaggerated result is typical
- Anything implying Meta or Facebook endorsement or affiliation
- Adult content, weapons, illegal products or services, tobacco, recreational drugs
- Discriminatory language
- Symbol or spacing tricks to slip past filters

## 3. Special Ad Categories

If the offer is in one of these, flag it at the top of the deliverable before the copy. Meta requires a Special Ad Category declaration in Ads Manager, which restricts targeting (no age, gender or zip-code targeting).

- **Credit:** loans, credit cards, financial assistance, buy-now-pay-later
- **Employment:** job postings, hiring, recruitment
- **Housing:** real estate, rentals, mortgages
- **Social issues, elections or politics**

Avoid copy that implies eligibility tied to age or life stage ("perfect for young professionals", "ideal for retirees"), since that contradicts the restricted targeting. If the user is unsure whether their offer qualifies, say so and ask them to confirm with whoever runs the ad account.

## 4. Earnings and business outcome claims

Coaching, "make money" and income offers get extra scrutiny.

- Never state or imply a specific, guaranteed income ("Make $10k/month", "Guaranteed 6-figure business").
- A testimonial number ("I made $50k in my first month") must be framed as one person's disclosed result, never as typical, and only used if the user confirms it is real and documented.
- Prefer directional outcome language: "more consistent leads", "a predictable pipeline", "a business that runs without you".

## 5. Health, wellness and before/after

- No claims of curing, treating or guaranteeing a health outcome.
- No before/after framing that implies an atypical result is standard.
- No diagnostic language that says the viewer has a condition. Reframe around the symptom or situation.

## 6. Formatting

- No ALL CAPS beyond a single emphasized word.
- No more than one exclamation point or symbol per sentence.
- No fake Facebook UI elements in copy (fake notification icons, fake "Like" references).

## Pre-submission checklist

- [ ] No line asserts or implies a personal attribute about the viewer
- [ ] No guaranteed or unrealistic outcome language
- [ ] Any specific result or testimonial number is real, disclosed as one person's result and user-approved
- [ ] Special Ad Category flagged if the offer is credit, employment, housing or political
- [ ] No implied Meta or Facebook endorsement
- [ ] No discriminatory language
- [ ] No excessive caps or punctuation
- [ ] Health and wellness offers make no diagnostic, curative or guaranteed claims

If any item fails, rewrite before delivery.
