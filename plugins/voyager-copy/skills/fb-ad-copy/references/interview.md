# Facebook Ad Interview

Follow `../../copy-foundations/references/interview-protocol.md`. Ask only what is not already known.

## Round 1 (always)

Post these together, numbered, and invite shorthand replies.

1. **What is the campaign goal?**
   a) Leads  b) Sales  c) Webinar or event registrations  d) Book a call  e) Awareness  f) Traffic
2. **Who are we talking to?**
   a) Cold (never heard of us)  b) Warm (engaged or visited)  c) Retargeting (downloaded, registered or booked)
3. **What are we promoting, and what happens after the click?** (For example: "free 15-minute audit, opens a booking page.")
4. **What is the ad format?**
   a) Static image  b) Video  c) Carousel

## Round 2 (only what the answers trigger)

| Trigger | Ask |
|---|---|
| Goal: leads | What is the lead magnet, and what does the person get on the next step? |
| Goal: sales | Price point, any guarantee, and the main objection people raise. |
| Goal: awareness | What is the one thing the audience should remember? |
| Temperature: warm or retargeting | Which page, ad or asset did they already see or use? |
| Always | **Tone framework:** a) Pattern Interrupt  b) Aspirational  c) Challenge-Led  d) Social Proof, or "you choose". Give a one-line description of each if the user is unsure. |
| Always | **Proof:** any results, testimonials or numbers we can use? (Real and approved only.) |
| Always | **Compliance:** does the offer fall in a Special Ad Category (housing, credit, employment, social issues or politics), or does it make income or health claims? |

Tone framework one-liners for the question:
- **Pattern Interrupt:** calls out the paradox, achievement without the expected payoff.
- **Aspirational:** validates their success, then reframes what comes next.
- **Challenge-Led:** names the plateau and positions a system as the fix.
- **Social Proof:** reframes the audience as successful but overstretched.

## Required fields (must be answered or assumed)

Goal, temperature, offer and post-click action, ad format, compliance status.

Nice to have: tone framework (default: 6-beat), proof (default: none used, no numbers invented), brand voice (default: plain and direct).

## Confirmation summary template

```
Here is what I will write:
- Deliverable: Facebook ad package (1 primary text, 5 headlines, 1 description, CTA)
- Goal: <goal>
- Audience: <temperature and who>
- Offer and next step: <offer, what happens after the click>
- Format: <static/video/carousel>
- Angle: <framework or 6-beat, hook direction>
- Proof: <what will be used, or none>
- Compliance: <no flags / Special Ad Category flag / claim restrictions>
- Assumptions: <list, or none>

Reply yes to write it, or tell me what to change.
```
