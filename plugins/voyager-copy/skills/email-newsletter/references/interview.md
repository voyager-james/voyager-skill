# Email and Newsletter Interview

Follow `../../copy-foundations/references/interview-protocol.md`. Ask only what is not already known.

## Round 1 (always)

Post these together, numbered, and invite shorthand replies.

1. **What type of email is it?**
   a) Newsletter  b) Promo broadcast  c) Welcome sequence  d) Nurture sequence  e) Launch or webinar sequence  f) Re-engagement
2. **What is the goal?**
   a) Educate  b) Drive a click  c) Book a call  d) Sell  e) Reactivate
3. **Who receives it, and how warm are they?** (List or segment, and whether they are new subscribers, engaged readers, customers or gone quiet.)
4. **What is the topic or offer, and what is the one action the reader should take?**

## Round 2 (only what the answers trigger)

| Trigger | Ask |
|---|---|
| Sequence (welcome, nurture, launch) | How many emails, how many days between them, and what triggers the start (opt-in, purchase, event date)? |
| Newsletter | Is there a fixed format to follow, or is this a one-off? Any story, lesson or example to include? |
| Promo broadcast or launch | Is there a real deadline or limited supply (real only)? Price? Proof to use? |
| Always | **Sender:** who is it from, and what voice? a) Personal (first person, founder or expert)  b) Brand (we)  c) Expert (authoritative teacher) |
| Always | **Length:** a) Short (under 300 words)  b) Standard (300 to 500)  c) Long-form (500 to 800) |

## Required fields (must be answered or assumed)

Email type, goal, recipients and warmth, topic or offer, the single action.

Nice to have: sender voice (default: personal, conversational), length (default: standard), proof (default: none used, none invented), deadline (default: none).

## Confirmation summary template

```
Here is what I will write:
- Deliverable: <newsletter / promo email / N-email sequence>
- Goal: <goal>
- Recipients: <segment and warmth>
- Topic or offer: <topic or offer>
- One action: <CTA>
- Sender and voice: <who, voice>
- Length: <band>
- Timing (sequences): <count, spacing, trigger>
- Proof and deadline: <what will be used, or none>
- Assumptions: <list, or none>

Reply yes to write it, or tell me what to change.
```
